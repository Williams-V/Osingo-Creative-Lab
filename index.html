Dennis Osingo

Multimedia creative · Photographer · Videographer · Digital content strategist

Every moment has a story. Our job is to help you tell it well.

Dennis Osingo is a Kenyan multimedia creative and digital marketing specialist with experience across photography, videography, social media content creation and digital communication. With a background in Journalism & Mass Communication, he combines storytelling with practical digital strategy — creating content that isn't simply visually appealing, but intentional.

Based in Nairobi, Kenya, Osingo Creative Lab works with businesses, organizations, brands, events and individuals looking to communicate through powerful visual storytelling.
